# MLAPSE WEBSITE
NGO Website for people living with siability in eswatini. Mavalela / Likhetseni Association for Persons Living with Disability in Eswatini (MLAPSE) is a registered non-governmental organization(NGO) formed to advocate for empower, and uplift the lives of people living with disabilities in Eswatini. The association was established in response to the growing need to adress systematic enequalities, limited access to information and social exclusion faced by individuals with disabilities.MLAPSE focuses on building inclusive communities through advocacy, education, and technology-driven accessibility initiatives.
